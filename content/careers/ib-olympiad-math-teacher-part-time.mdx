## Role | Teaching IB and Olympiad math online

Many students find advanced math intimidating, and even strong students can hit a wall when IB exams or olympiad problems ask them to think in unfamiliar ways. The right teacher can turn that pressure into confidence and genuine curiosity.

You will have the opportunity to help students who want to go further in math, mastering the IB curriculum and taking on olympiad-level problem solving, while making the process joyful rather than stressful.

### Some more details

- Teach students math over Google Meet/Zoom, from age 8 up to grade 12.
- Students in this role typically fall into two groups:
  - **IB Mathematics** (MYP and DP, including Analysis & Approaches and Applications & Interpretation at SL and HL)
  - **Math olympiad and competition preparation** (e.g. SASMO, SMO, AMC, Math Kangaroo and national olympiads)
- As a teacher, your role will be to guide students to understand the WHY behind math concepts and build strong problem-solving strategies, in a way that they can proudly say "I did this myself" or "we did this together". This will require a lot of patience, empathy, and the skill of coaching. You'll be problem solving and learning together along the way, and often our teachers learn a lot from their students.

## Who you are

**We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply.**

### Minimum requirements

- You can confidently teach IB Mathematics up to grade 12 (DP level), and are familiar with the IB curriculum and assessment style.
- You have experience teaching, coaching or competing in math olympiads or competitions.
- You have a lot of patience and a passion for working with kids and teenagers.
- You have an energetic personality that can hold a student's attention for 1 hour.
- Must be able to commit to at least 5 hours of teaching per week.
- Have a strong and reliable internet connection.

### Preferred

- You've taught IB Mathematics before (bonus if you've been an IB examiner or moderator)
- You've prepared students for olympiads or competitions, or competed yourself
- You know how to code in Python (not required, but a nice to have)
- You already have your own paid AI service (ChatGPT, Claude, or similar)
- You own an iPad/tablet
